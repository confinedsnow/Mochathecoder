About Me

Hello, I'm an aspiring QA tester. I identify game-breaking bugs, document edge cases, and help developers ship more stable experiences — especially on Roblox.

Outside of testing, I enjoy interacting with people in real life, learning more about Linux, and picking up new languages.

I choose to work for a low price because many creators are just starting out, and I want to give them a chance to get feedback from a fresh perspective. I strongly believe that testing and feedback should be accessible, especially for smaller developers who may not have the resources yet.
